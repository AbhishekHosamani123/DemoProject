# Functional Requirements Document (FRD)

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Purpose

The purpose of this Functional Requirements Document (FRD) is to define **detailed functional requirements** for the Sales Data Analytics System.  
This document explains *what the system will do* to meet business and product requirements.

### 1.2 Scope

This FRD covers functionalities related to:
- Sales analysis
- Profit tracking
- Customer insights
- Product performance
- Payment mode analysis
- Reporting using historical sales data

---

## 2. System Overview

The Sales Data Analytics System ingests historical sales data and transforms it into interactive dashboards and reports.  
The system is designed for business users to analyze performance without technical expertise.

---

## 3. User Roles

| Role          | Description                       |
| ------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Business User | Views dashboards and reports      |
| Sales Manager | Analyzes sales trends and regions |
| Finance User  | Reviews profit and loss           |
| Admin         | Manages data refresh and access   |

---

## 4. Functional Requirements

### FR-1: Data Ingestion & Validation

- The system shall import sales order and order detail datasets.
- The system shall validate records for missing or invalid values.
- The system shall use Order ID as a unique identifier to join datasets.

---

### FR-2: Sales Performance Analysis

- The system shall calculate total sales revenue.
- The system shall display sales trends by month, state, and city.
- The system shall allow filtering by date range and location.

---

### FR-3: Profit Analysis

- The system shall calculate total and average profit.
- The system shall identify loss-making orders and products.
- The system shall display profit by category and sub-category.

---

### FR-4: Product Analysis

- The system shall display performance by product category and sub-category.
- The system shall show quantity sold per product.
- The system shall identify top and bottom-performing products.

---

### FR-5: Customer Analysis

- The system shall identify top customers by revenue and profit.
- The system shall display customer contribution to total sales.

---

### FR-6: Payment Mode Analysis

- The system shall display sales and profit by payment mode.
- The system shall compare COD and digital payment performance.

---

### FR-7: Dashboards & Visualization

- The system shall provide interactive dashboards.
- The system shall allow filtering by date, region, product, and payment mode.
- The system shall update visuals dynamically based on selected filters.

---

### FR-8: Reporting & Export

- The system shall allow export of reports in Excel and PDF formats.
- The system shall generate summary and detailed reports.

---

### FR-9: Access Control

- The system shall provide role-based read-only access.
- The system shall restrict data modification capabilities.

---

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements (Summary)

- Reports shall load within 3–5 seconds.
- The system shall support increasing data volume.
- The interface shall be easy to use for non-technical users.

---

## 6. Assumptions

- Sales data provided is accurate and complete.
- All data is historical and static.

---

## 7. Constraints

- No real-time analytics or live integrations.
- No data creation or editing features.

---

## 8. Success Criteria

- Accurate and consistent analytical outputs
- Reduced manual reporting effort
- Improved visibility into sales and profit trends
