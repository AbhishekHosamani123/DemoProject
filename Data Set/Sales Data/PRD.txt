## 2. Purpose of the Document

The purpose of this Business Requirements Document (BRD) is to clearly define the business needs, objectives, functional requirements, and non-functional requirements for the **Sales Data Analysis and Reporting System**.

This system will utilize historical sales data to provide insights into:

- Sales performance  
- Customer behavior  
- Profitability  
- Operational efficiency  

to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 3. Business Background

The organization maintains sales transaction data across multiple regions, customers, product categories, and payment modes.

Currently, insights are derived manually or through limited reporting, which is:

- Time-consuming  
- Prone to inconsistencies  

There is a need for a **structured, automated, and scalable solution** to analyze sales data efficiently.

---

## 4. Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance across time, location, and product categories
- Identify profitable and non-profitable products and regions
- Understand customer purchasing patterns
- Track order volume, revenue, profit, and quantity sold
- Support strategic decisions with accurate dashboards and reports

---

## 5. Scope

### 5.1 In-Scope

- Analysis of sales orders and order details
- Reporting based on:
  - Order Date
  - State and City
  - Customer Name
  - Product Category and Sub-Category
  - Payment Mode
  - Revenue, Profit, and Quantity
- Dashboard and report generation
- Data visualization for business users

### 5.2 Out-of-Scope

- Real-time transaction processing
- Integration with external ERP or CRM systems
- Data entry or modification features

## 7. Data Sources

The system will use the following datasets:

### 7.1 Orders Dataset

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Customer Name
- State
- City

### 7.2 Order Details Dataset

- Order ID
- Amount (Sales Revenue)
- Profit
- Quantity
- Category
- Sub-Category
- Payment Mode

---

## 8. Functional Requirements

### 8.1 Data Integration

- The system shall merge Orders and Order Details datasets using **Order ID** as the primary key.
- The system shall validate data consistency and completeness.

### 8.2 Sales Analysis

- The system shall calculate total sales amount by:
  - Date
  - State
  - City
  - Category
- The system shall provide:
  - Monthly summaries
  - Quarterly summaries
  - Yearly summaries

### 8.3 Profit Analysis

- The system shall calculate total and average profit per:
  - Category
  - Sub-Category
- The system shall identify loss-making orders and products.

### 8.4 Customer Analysis

- The system shall identify top customers by:
  - Revenue
  - Profit
- The system shall analyze repeat versus single-time customers.

### 8.5 Product Analysis

- The system shall show performance by Category and Sub-Category.
- The system shall display quantity sold by product type.

### 8.6 Payment Mode Analysis

- The system shall analyze sales and profit distribution by Payment Mode, including:
  - Cash on Delivery (COD)
  - EMI
  - Credit Card
  - Other payment methods

### 8.7 Reporting and Dashboards

- The system shall provide interactive dashboards.
- The system shall allow filtering by:
  - Date
  - Region
  - Category
  - Payment Mode
- The system shall export reports in:
  - Excel format
  - PDF format

---

## 9. Non-Functional Requirements

### 9.1 Performance

- Reports should load within **3–5 seconds** for standard queries.

### 9.2 Security

- Access should be role-based.
- Data should be read-only for business users.

### 9.3 Usability

- Dashboards should be easy to understand for non-technical users.
- Visuals should be clear and consistent.

### 9.4 Scalability

- The system should support future data growth.

---

## 10. Assumptions

- The provided sales data is accurate and complete.
- Order ID uniquely identifies each transaction.
- All monetary values are in a single currency.

---

## 11. Constraints

- Analysis limited to historical data only.
- No live data updates.

---

| ------------------ | ------------------- | ------------------------ |
